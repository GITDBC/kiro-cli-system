# Usage beyond plan limits

> Understanding how you can continue using Kiro beyond your plan's usage limits.

Add-on credits let you keep working after you've used your plan's monthly credit allocation. They're available on all paid tiers (Pro, Pro+, Pro Max, and Power) at $0.04 per credit, billed immediately when you purchase them.

**ℹ️ Info:** You can't purchase add-on credits on the **Kiro Free** tier. When you hit your credit limit, you must wait until the next month for your credits to reset.

## Purchasing add-on credits

To purchase add-on credits, navigate to the [Kiro portal](https://app.kiro.dev/account/usage) and select **Buy add-on credits**. This opens a panel where you enter the number of credits to purchase.

- **Minimum purchase:** $5 (125 credits)
- **Maximum per pack:** $100
- **Pack limit:** Up to 5 add-on credit packs at a time

When you select "Next", you're taken to the Stripe portal to enter payment details and complete the purchase. Once payment succeeds, the purchased credits appear on your account page.

![Kiro portal showing the add-on credits purchase panel](https://kiro.dev/images/billing/add-on-credits.png)

## Using add-on credits

- **Display:** When you log in to Kiro IDE or Kiro CLI, purchased add-on credits are combined with your plan credits in the usage display. For example, a Pro plan with 125 add-on credits shows 1,125 total available credits.
- **Consumption order:** Kiro uses your plan credits first. Add-on credits are only consumed after plan credits are exhausted.
- **Expiry and rollover:** Add-on credits expire 12 months from the date of purchase. Unused credits roll over month to month until they're used or reach their expiry date. The earliest-expiring pack is consumed first.
- **When both run out:** If you exhaust both plan credits and all add-on credits, Kiro pauses usage until you purchase more add-on credits or your plan resets at the start of the next billing cycle.
