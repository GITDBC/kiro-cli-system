# Managing proactive usage notifications

> Stay informed about your credit usage with automated notifications so you can take action before running out of credits.

Kiro notifies you when you're approaching your credit limit, giving you time to upgrade your plan before usage is paused.

![Billing Notification Setting](https://kiro.dev/images/notifications-billing.png)

## Notification types

Kiro sends usage notifications to help you stay on top of your credit consumption:

### Low resource notifications

When you reach 80% of your monthly credit usage, Kiro sends a low resource notification with options based on your current plan:
- **Free tier users** receive suggestions to upgrade to a paid tier for continued access, with a reminder that credits reset monthly and add-on credits aren't available.
- **Paid tier users** receive different options depending on their situation:
    - If you **can upgrade**, you'll be prompted to upgrade for more credits or purchase add-on credits.

**💡 Tip:** If you consistently reach your credit limit early in your billing cycle, consider upgrading to a higher tier to ensure uninterrupted productivity.

## Managing notification preferences

By default, billing notifications are enabled. You can disable these notifications in your settings:

1. Open Settings: **Command Palette** (`⌘`/`Ctrl` + `Shift` + `P`)
2. Search for `Notifications: Billing`
3. Unselect **Notifications: Billing** to disable billing-related notifications.
